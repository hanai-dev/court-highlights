Common questions

A court shows "Camera offline"

Check the camera's power and network cable. Recording starts again by itself within seconds of the camera coming back.

Someone asks for footage after an incident

Open Footage, find the court and time, and choose Hold. Held footage is kept for up to 60 days. The box keeps everything else for 4 hours only.

Players want a clip kept longer

Open Clips and choose Keep. Kept clips stay for 1 year.

Signs for the courts

Open Court signs and print one sign per court for the net posts.
