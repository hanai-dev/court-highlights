Easy Expansion Options

The EQ14 features dual M.2 PCle 3.0 SSD slots supporting up to 8TB of fast internal storage (The retail unit ships with a SATA III SSD), it also supports single-channel DDR4 3200MHz memory of up to 16GB.

32dB Near-Silent Operation

Air intake from the bottom is so efficient at dissipating the heat that EQ14 needs very low fan speed to stay cool and stable, ensuring near-silent operation. Since there are no vents on the top and side panels, the mini PC can now have a more refreshing design.

Seamlessly Connect with Intel Unison

EQ14 supports connecting mobile devices seamlessly with the Intel unison app. For example, you will be able to view smartphone photo galleries on your PC, extend your PC screen with a tablet, make and receive phone calls from your PC, transfer files from phone or tablet to your PC, send and receive text messages from your PC, manage device notifications from a single source and so on.
